How it plays

Everything is on his phone

Messages tell the story. The rest of the phone fills in what nobody says out loud.

Messages

Tap to reveal each text. A green dot shows who just messaged you.

Social

Rina's posts appear the moment she makes them in the story.

Phone

Missed calls and a voicemail she left at midnight.

Notes

Kai's private notes, where he tries to make sense of what he wants.

Browser

His search history at 3am says more than his texts.

Choices

Pick Kai's replies. Some change what Rina feels about him, one changes everything.
